1 · Material

Thickness, bend length and radius accept decimals or fractions – 0.125, 1/8, 3/16, 1 1/2 – and an optional unit (3.2mm, 1/8 in). Thickness 2 mm and bend length 1000 mm are placeholders – set the real values. Switching units converts the numbers already entered. Strength and springback presets are typical values only; use your mill-cert numbers.

3 · Tooling

Add or remove a tool
Tools you add are kept in this browser on this device only. Send me the JSON (or the controller tool screens) and I will build them into the page for good. Punch height, angle and max load are required; the outline is only needed for the collision check.

4 · Bend sequence

Auto tries every order and both sides (front / back) at every step against the punch and die outlines, force, flange, angle, height and throat. It prefers fewer warnings, then fewer turn-overs of the part. "Side" is where the start of the profile (segment 1) goes: front = operator side. Manual lets you set the order and side for each step.
Machine & tool values (filled from the tool selection – override if needed)
Machine: C500-6100 row of the MVD catalog. Tools: BIU-023 (86°, R1, H 163, 0.65 t/cm) and ES-V22 4-V die (85°, 60×60, 0.80 t/cm) from your controller tool screens. Leave the holder heights blank if unknown – the open-height check then says it is incomplete.
Tool outlines used for collision (filled from the tool selection)
Digitised from the MODEL screens on your controller, so expect about ±1.5 mm. Relief toward the back matches your side-on photo of the punch; tick the box if it is mounted the other way. Die outline ignores the 2.5 mm shoulder radius.
Assumptions: air bend; force F = (S²·2·TS·L)/(1.4·V) (a 90° air-bend figure); minimum flange ≈ 0.7·V; air-bend inside radius ≈ 0.16·V; segment lengths used as typed. Back gauge: the Universal finger from the controller (10 mm ledge, 10 mm stop, ledge reaches 20.6 mm in front of the stop face); the finger sits on the die top when the part ends over the die and on its ledge when the part reaches past the die. Dies other than ES-V22 are digitised from controller dimensions (shoulder radii ignored); OZU/ES-V16/V35/V50 max loads come from the controller's resistance (t/cm). Punches 9.75in GooseNeck, BIU-226 and BIU-004 are built from the controller's GENERIC model dimensions; BIU-021 is digitised from the controller's picture (approximate). OZU-805 shows 31.50 t/cm on the controller – 1180 kN/m is used instead; Rule: at every bend the bigger part (longer developed length on its side of the bend) goes to the front of the machine, never the back. This is a screening tool – confirm borderline results on the controller and with the tooling data.
